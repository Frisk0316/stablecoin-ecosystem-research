Transparency

USDPT reserve attestations

USDPT is Western Union’s U.S. dollar-denominated stablecoin issued by Anchorage Digital Bank, designed to modernize cross-border transfers.

Reserve attestations

USDPT reserve holdings are fully disclosed on a monthly basis. Attestation reports are provided by a Big Four independent third-party accounting firm. The reports are prepared in accordance with the attestation standards established by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA).
